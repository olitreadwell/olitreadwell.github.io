Writing me a recommendation? Thank you.

The hardest part is starting. Pick any two or three of the prompts below. They're shortcuts, not a checklist. The button further down opens an email draft to me with your answers pre-filled. With your permission I'll post it on my site or on LinkedIn.

Want a sense of what other people have written? See all my LinkedIn recommendations , or read the Derrick Parkhurst example further down.

Nothing is sent or stored until you click send.

Your details (optional)

Filling these in personalises the email subject and intro. Both fields are optional.

Prompts to choose from

  1. A specific moment. Describe one project or moment where my work made a measurable difference. What changed because I was on it?
  2. How I work with others. What's it like to be on my team or in a code review with me? Communication, mentoring, how I handle disagreement.
  3. Strength you'd hire for. If you were building a team, what's the role you'd put me in first, and why? Senior IC, frontend lead, mentor, anything else.
  4. Something less obvious. What's a quality of mine that doesn't show up on a resume? Curiosity, calm under pressure, a knack for clarifying ambiguous requirements?
  5. One-sentence recommendation. If a hiring manager asked you in one sentence whether to hire me, what would you say?

An example: Derrick Parkhurst

Derrick and I worked together at Condé Nast on paywalls and shared UI for The New Yorker, Vanity Fair, and Wired. He wrote this in December 2018.
