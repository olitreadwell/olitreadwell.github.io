Writing me a recommendation? Thank you.

The hardest part is starting. Pick any two or three of the prompts below. They're shortcuts, not a checklist. The button further down opens an email draft to me with your answers pre-filled. With your permission I'll post it on my site or on LinkedIn.

Want a sense of what other people have written? See all my LinkedIn recommendations , or read the Derrick Parkhurst example further down.

Nothing is sent or stored until you click send.

Write a recommendation

Every field is optional. As you fill things in, the preview at the bottom assembles into a recommendation you can email me or paste into LinkedIn. Your draft is saved in your browser so you can come back to it.

A PR review, pair-programming session, meeting, or any concrete moment where Oli's work made a measurable difference. What changed because he was on it?

What's it like to be on Oli's team or in a code review with him? Communication, mentoring, how he handles disagreement.

If you were building a team, what's the role you'd put Oli in first, and why?

What's a quality of his that doesn't show up on a resume? Curiosity, calm under pressure, a knack for clarifying ambiguous requirements?

How does Oli compare to other engineers you've worked with at his level?

What kind of work or responsibility would you happily hand to Oli without checking in?

If a hiring manager asked you in one sentence whether to hire Oli, what would you say?
